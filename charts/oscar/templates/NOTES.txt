OSCAR stores configuration, application data and SD inputs on separate PVCs.
Back up all three: /config alone does NOT include the nested data/SD mounts.
Managed generation-v2 PVCs are retained (orphaned) on uninstall.
Reuse them through explicit persistence.<role>.existingClaim values; never
remove or rewrite another release's ownership metadata to force adoption.

The service uses HTTP port 3000. Both public routes are disabled by default.
For remote access provide HTTPS, authentication, WebSocket upgrade support
and suitable long-lived connection timeouts on your existing proxy/controller.
See docs/chart.md for backup, routing and safe reinstallation examples.
